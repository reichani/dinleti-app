# Okurio Vertical Reading Strategy v1.0

## Strategic choice

Okurio will not compete by trying to out-feature horizontal accessibility suites. Font choice, spacing, TTS, reading rulers and the browser extension are important access capabilities, but they are not the premium moat.

Okurio's chosen category is **Kişiselleştirilmiş Okuma Platformu / Adaptive Reading Platform for Schools**.

## Customer job

For the learner: receive a text and support pattern that fits the current reading path without being labelled or medicalised.

For the teacher: scale differentiated reading across a class without manually reformatting every text or configuring every learner from scratch.

For the school: operate one governed Turkish reading system spanning content, accessibility, reading paths, teacher workflow and progress insight.

## Value architecture

### Commodity access layer
- Font and spacing controls
- Themes and reading ruler
- Text-to-speech
- Browser extension

These are included in the product and should not lead pricing or positioning.

### Differentiation layer
- Age-based and stage-based reading paths
- Turkish age-fit word support
- Curated, quality-governed content library
- Reading-level policy and content eligibility
- Adaptive support profiles
- Human content-quality review governance

### Monetization layer
- Student groups
- Teacher workflow and assignments
- Progress and usage insights
- School content management
- Onboarding and support
- Enterprise integration/SSO where required

## Competitive rule

Do not build a feature race against Microsoft, Read&Write or other horizontal assistive tools. Prefer vertical depth in Turkish reading development and classroom implementation.

## Data moat hypothesis

The long-term proprietary asset is the governed relationship between:

`student profile × reading path × support pattern × content characteristics × observed outcome signals`

No diagnostic or therapeutic claim is implied. Outcome signals must be framed as product/learning usage indicators unless validated for stronger claims.

## Pricing architecture

School pricing follows **annual platform fee + active-student band**. Teacher accounts and the browser extension are included. Pilot is a separate paid programme. SSO, custom integrations and custom content programmes are enterprise add-ons.

## Product copy rule

Lead with:
- the right reading path,
- Turkish content and word support,
- level-fit content,
- personalized support,
- teacher-scalable workflow.

Do not lead with:
- OpenDyslexic/font,
- TTS,
- spacing,
- extension availability.

Those remain useful access capabilities inside the system.
