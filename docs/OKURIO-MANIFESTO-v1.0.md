# OKURIO PRODUCT MANIFESTO v1.0

## Status

Authoritative product constitution.

This manifesto governs product strategy, content, design, accessibility, analytics and release decisions. If a roadmap item, feature request, story concept or commercial request conflicts with this manifesto, the manifesto prevails unless the Product Owner formally approves a new version.

## 1. Mission

Okurio helps children build a more accessible, enjoyable and sustainable reading experience.

Okurio supports reading through clear design, short original stories, attention-aware interaction and age-appropriate contextual word support.

## 2. Reading First

Every feature must answer one question:

> Does this make reading easier, clearer, calmer or more sustainable?

Features that do not materially improve the reading experience are postponed or rejected.

## 3. Accessibility Before Feature Volume

Readability, focus, cognitive load, consistency and safe mobile use take priority over adding more controls or decorative functionality.

Okurio does not measure product maturity by feature count.

## 4. Story-Driven Learning

Okurio supports development through stories and reading experiences.

Okurio is not:

- a therapy platform
- a clinical intervention
- a diagnostic product
- a treatment product
- a behaviour scoring system
- a psychological assessment tool
- an AI teacher or AI tutor

## 5. Social-Emotional Reading Scope

Okurio may support the following only through original, age-appropriate stories, character situations and optional reflection prompts:

- recognising emotions
- naming and expressing emotions
- understanding another person's feelings
- perspective taking
- empathy
- noticing impulsive reactions
- pausing before acting
- waiting and turn-taking
- coping with frustration
- repairing misunderstandings
- asking before assuming intent

The product must never claim to treat ADHD, dyslexia, emotional dysregulation or any clinical condition.

## 6. No Clinical Claims

Okurio does not diagnose, screen, treat or provide clinical recommendations.

Product and marketing language must not include claims such as:

- treats ADHD
- improves a diagnosed disorder
- provides therapy
- assesses emotional health
- replaces a psychologist, clinician, teacher or special education professional

Approved language includes:

- supports accessible reading
- supports attention-aware reading experiences
- supports social-emotional awareness through stories
- helps children reflect on feelings and perspectives
- provides dyslexia-friendly reading controls

## 7. Child Dignity First

No story, prompt, feature or message may shame, label, punish or pathologise a child.

Characters may make mistakes, feel overwhelmed or react quickly, but the narrative must preserve dignity and show learning without humiliation.

## 8. Experience Leadership Council

The Okurio Experience Leadership Council owns the integrity of the reading experience.

### Accessibility Product Owner

Owns:

- readability
- contrast
- keyboard and touch access
- focus visibility
- mobile accessibility
- reading ruler and line focus usability

May block a release that reduces accessibility.

### Dyslexia Product Experience Lead

Owns:

- sentence and paragraph complexity
- visual reading load
- spacing and font behaviour
- glossary design
- reading fluency risks
- dyslexia-friendly content review

May block content or features that create avoidable decoding difficulty.

### ADHD Product Experience Lead

Owns:

- cognitive load
- screen complexity
- task clarity
- attention transitions
- story rhythm
- interruption and distraction risks

May block content or features that create avoidable attention overload.

### Social-Emotional Reading Lead

Owns:

- emotion representation
- empathy and perspective-taking content
- impulse-regulation story framing
- non-clinical language
- dignity and safeguarding review

May block social-emotional content that becomes diagnostic, therapeutic, moralising, shaming or clinically suggestive.

### Content Product Owner

Owns:

- story quality
- character consistency
- age-band suitability
- target vocabulary
- originality and copyright safety
- final editorial approval

## 9. Release Governance

A planned release cannot be approved only because it builds successfully.

Relevant experience leads must approve the release according to scope:

- Accessibility Product Owner: every release
- Dyslexia Product Experience Lead: every reading or content release
- ADHD Product Experience Lead: every reading, navigation or content release
- Social-Emotional Reading Lead: every social-emotional story, prompt or feature
- Content Product Owner: every new or changed story

A lead may record:

- approved
- approved with conditions
- changes required
- not applicable

Unresolved "changes required" decisions block the release.

## 10. Content Principles

Every pilot story must:

- remain centred on a readable story rather than a lesson lecture
- preserve character dignity
- avoid clinical terminology
- avoid diagnosing the reader or character
- show choices and consequences without punishment-led messaging
- use optional reflection rather than compulsory testing
- use one primary developmental theme per story
- include three to eight approved target words
- meet the content quality rules in the PRD

## 11. Reflection Prompt Rule

A story may end with one optional reflection prompt, such as:

- Sence Mino ne hissetti?
- Oki başka ne yapabilirdi?
- Bu durumda önce ne sorabilirdi?

Reflection prompts must not score, classify or judge the child.

## 12. Product Boundary Test

Before work starts, the owner must answer:

1. Is reading still the primary experience?
2. Is the developmental support delivered through story or reading interaction?
3. Does the feature avoid diagnosis, therapy and clinical recommendation?
4. Does it preserve child dignity?
5. Is the cognitive and visual load appropriate?
6. Have the relevant Experience Leads reviewed it?

If any answer is no, the item does not enter implementation without redesign and Product Owner approval.

## 13. Strategic Positioning

Approved positioning:

> Okurio is an ADHD- and dyslexia-friendly accessible reading platform that supports language, attention and social-emotional awareness through original stories and thoughtful reading experiences.

This positioning does not authorise clinical or therapeutic claims.
